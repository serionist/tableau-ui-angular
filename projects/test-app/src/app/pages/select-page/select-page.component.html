Select Dropdown

A select picks one value, or several, from a dropdown list of options.

Import

Import TableauUiSelectModule from tableau-ui-angular/select for tab-single-select and tab-multi-select, and TableauUiCommonModule from tableau-ui-angular/common for tab-option. Each is a combobox with a listbox, for the keyboard and screen readers.

Options

A tab-option takes its text, hint and icon as values or as templates, and can be disabled. Without a form, bind [value] and (valueChange); this one shows a snack on each change.

How the value shows

selectedValueWrapMode wraps or cuts the value short, [selectedValueTemplateContext] leaves out its icon, selectedCheckIconLocation puts the check on the left, the right or nowhere, and [showChevron], [allowClear] and [selectedItemHighlight] turn their parts on or off. A form field's prefix and suffix sit around the select.

Reactive forms

Bind [formControl] or formControlName. The control is touched when the user leaves the select, and a disabled control disables it.

Multiple values

tab-multi-select holds an array. [multipleSelectionMaxItemsListed] caps how many it lists before multipleSelectionNumberSelectedTemplate says how many there are, and [dropdownOptionsContainerCss] styles the list.

A search in the dropdown

A tab-prefix and a tab-suffix inside the select sit above and below its options: here a search box, a loading spinner and the count.

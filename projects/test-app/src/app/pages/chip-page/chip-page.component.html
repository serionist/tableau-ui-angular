Chips

A chip shows a short piece of information, such as a tag; the chip control holds a list of chips as its value.

Import

Import TableauUiChipModule from tableau-ui-angular/chip, and TableauUiChipControlModule and the ChipData type from tableau-ui-angular/chip-control.

Chips

tab-chip shows its content. [selected] highlights it, and its font size sets its size. [allowRemove] adds a remove button, with [removeTooltip] for its tooltip. The button emits (removed), and the app takes the chip away.

Chip control

tab-chip-control shows a chip for each key, drawn by the template marked tabChipTemplate. The template's context has the chip's data, whether it is selected, removable or disabled, and the control, whose remove() takes a chip away.

Input What it does
[(keys)] The chips' keys, which identify them
[(values)] Each chip's key and the value it shows, as ChipData; setting the values sets the keys, and the other way round
getValuesByKeys Required. Looks up the values of keys set without them; a spinner shows until it answers
[disabled] Disables the control and its chips

From the control's text box, Backspace and the left arrow select a chip, and Backspace or Delete removes it.

Reactive forms

Bind [formControl] or formControlName: the form's value is the keys, and the control looks up what each one shows. The control is touched when its text box loses focus, and a disabled control disables it.

Accessibility

  • A chip's remove button is named Remove and the chip's content, such as Remove Berlin. Its content's decorative icons are left out.
  • In the chip control, focus stays in the text box. Backspace and the left arrow highlight a chip, the left and right arrows move the highlight, and Backspace or Delete removes the highlighted chip.
  • A hidden polite live region in the control says what those keys do: Berlin, highlighted. Backspace or Delete removes it when a key highlights a chip, and Berlin removed when one removes it. A chip's words are its text, without decorative icons or its buttons.
  • The app names the control: a tab-label in its tab-form-field names its text box.

Form Fields

A form field wraps an input with a label, hints, errors, a prefix and a suffix.

Import

Import TableauUiFormFieldModule from tableau-ui-angular/form-field. The typed forms, FB and their pipes, are in TableauUiFormModule from tableau-ui-angular/form, and suggestions in TableauUiAutoCompleteModule from tableau-ui-angular/autocomplete.

Value binding

Without a form, bind [value] and listen to (input) or (change); the field here shows a snack on each change. tab-hint sits under the input, on the left or with type="suffix" on the right.

Suggestions

[tabAutoComplete] opens a tab-autocomplete under the input, with its options, and a prefix and a suffix of its own. It shows while it has one of them: with none, as when the text matches no option and there is no prefix or suffix, it stays closed, and Escape goes to the page. This one searches as the user types.

Reactive forms

Bind formControlName or [formControl]; a disabled control disables the field. These fields come from FB, whose controls give their value as value$.

Validation

Show a tab-error when the control has an error: formMeta and formHasError read it, by name or any.

Accessibility

The field names its control by the label and describes it by the hints and the errors; the app adds nothing.

The control gets How
Its name The tab-label: its for names a native input, textarea or select, and aria-labelledby names the host of a select, a list or a radio group, or a chip control's search input
Its description aria-describedby names the hints while they show, then the errors while they show
Its state aria-invalid="true" while an error shows
An announcement A hidden polite live region says an error that appears or changes while focus is in the field; one that appears while focus is elsewhere stays quiet

Ids the app puts in the control's own aria-describedby or aria-labelledby stay, with the field's after them.

On submit, move focus to the first invalid field. The fields say only the error of the field that has focus, so a submit that shows five errors stays quiet, and the user hears each error on reaching its field. Call markAllAsTouched() on the form, and once its errors show, focus the first element in it with aria-invalid="true".

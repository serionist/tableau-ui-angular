Nav Bar

A nav bar lists an app's pages down its side, and closes to a column of icons.

Import

Import TableauUiNavBarModule from tableau-ui-angular/nav-bar.

A nav bar

tab-nav-bar holds a tab-nav-bar-header, its buttons and a tab-nav-bar-footer. [(expanded)] binds whether it is open; closed, it shows each button's icon, with its text as a tooltip and as its name for screen readers.

Button input What it does
text Required. The button's text
link A router link, as routerLink takes it, through the app's router. Without one, the button is a button, and the app handles its click
[isActive] Marks the current page
[disabled] Stops the link or the click

This site's nav bar, on the left, uses links: its source is app.component.html.

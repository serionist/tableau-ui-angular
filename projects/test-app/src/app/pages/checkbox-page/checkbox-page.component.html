Checkbox

A checkbox selects or clears one option, with a value binding or a form control.

Import

Import TableauUiCheckboxModule from tableau-ui-angular/checkbox . Hints and errors, tab-hint and tab-error , come from TableauUiCommonModule in tableau-ui-angular/common .

Value binding

Bind [(value)] , and listen to (valueChange) for the user's toggles; each one here shows a snack. [loading] shows a spinner in place of the box.

Partial

The value 'partial' comes only from code. A click leaves it for valueAfterPartial , false by default.

Reactive forms

Bind formControlName or [formControl] . The control is touched when the user leaves the checkbox, and a disabled control disables it.

Expansion Panel

An expansion panel shows its content when the user opens it; in an accordion, at most one panel is open.

Import

Import TableauUiExpansionPanelModule from tableau-ui-angular/expansion-panel.

Panels

tab-expansion-panel-title is the panel's title; a title marked collapsed or expanded takes its place in that state. A click on the title, or on its arrow button, opens and closes the panel.

  • [(expanded)] binds whether the panel is open, as the checkbox here does for the first one.
  • [disabled] keeps it as it is.
  • [noHeaderHover] turns the title's hover colour off.
  • label names the arrow button in place of the title, as the last panel here does.
  • A button in the title keeps its click from reaching the title, or the click toggles the panel too.

Accordion

tab-accordion holds panels, and at most one is open: opening a panel closes the others.

Accessibility

  • The arrow button opens and closes the panel, from the keyboard too, and its aria-expanded says whether the panel is open.
  • The title names the arrow button, or Details without a title. A title that holds a control, such as a button, or more than the panel's name needs label: without it the button's name is the title's whole text, the control's included, and a search for the control by its name finds the arrow button too.

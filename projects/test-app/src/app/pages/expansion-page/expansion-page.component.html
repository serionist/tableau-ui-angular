Expansion Panel

An expansion panel shows its content when the user opens it; in an accordion, one panel is open at a time.

Import

Import TableauUiExpansionPanelModule from tableau-ui-angular/expansion-panel.

Panels

tab-expansion-panel-title is the panel's title; a title marked collapsed or expanded takes its place in that state. A click on the title, or on its arrow button, opens and closes the panel.

  • [(expanded)] binds whether the panel is open.
  • [disabled] keeps it as it is.
  • [noHeaderHover] turns the title's hover colour off.
  • A button in the title keeps its click from reaching the title, or the click toggles the panel too.

Accordion

tab-accordion holds panels and keeps one of them open: opening a panel closes the others.

Dialogs

A dialog shows information or asks the user to decide, above the page.

Import

Inject DialogService from tableau-ui-angular/dialog: one for the app, so Escape closes the top dialog. On the server it shows nothing, and a confirmation resolves false at once.

Confirmation with a message

openConfirmationMessageDialog() resolves true when the user accepts. Its colour styles the accept button, and autofocus focuses accept, cancel or neither.

Confirmation with a template

openConfirmationTemplateDialog() shows a template as the content, and takes the buttons' texts.

A component of its own

openModal() shows any component, with no buttons of its own. The component reads its data with injectDialogData() and closes with injectDialogRef(); closed$ gives the result.

Overlays in a modal

A tooltip, a snack, or a dialog the app opens with openDialog() or openTemplateDialog(), opened while a modal is open, goes into that modal: it sits above the modal's content and takes its clicks. A select's list, an autocomplete's suggestions and a menu sit after their element, so in the modal already. A modal that closes first closes the dialogs in it, top first, and the snacks go back to the page. A modal opened from a modal, a confirmation included, goes to the body above it, so a modal can open the next one and close itself.

$overlayHost() is where an overlay opened now belongs: the topmost modal's container, or null while no modal is open, for the body. An app that appends an overlay of its own to the body appends it to $overlayHost() ?? document.body instead. When the modal closes, the service moves what the app put in it on to the next host, the topmost modal still open or the body; the app removes what should go with the modal. The overlays are position: fixed in the modal's container, so a transform, filter or contain on .dialog-container would move and clip them.
